# Coca-Cola-Sales-Dashboard
This project presents an interactive Coca-Cola Sales Dashboard developed in Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance across products, regions, and sales channels. The dashboard provides stakeholders with a clear view of key business metrics and supports data-driven decision-making.

## Key Business Insights
Regional Performance
Abuja recorded the highest revenue of $99M.
Ibadan generated $94M in revenue.
Enugu and Lagos each contributed $92M.
Kano generated $90M.
Port Harcourt recorded the lowest revenue at $84M.

## Product Profitability
Minute Maid, Eva Water, and Diet Coke were the most profitable products, each generating approximately $12M profit.
Sprite, Schweppes, Fanta, and Coke Zero generated around $11M profit each.
Coca-Cola Classic recorded the lowest product profit at $10M.

## Sales Channel Performance
Retail contributed the highest share of units sold (26%).
Online sales accounted for 25%.
Distributors accounted for 25%.
Wholesale contributed 24%.

## Revenue Trends
Monthly revenue fluctuated between $43M and $49M.
Peak performance was achieved in March, May, July, and October.
Revenue remained relatively stable throughout the year, indicating consistent sales performance.

## Business Recommendation
Investigate factors driving Abuja's strong performance and replicate successful strategies in lower-performing regions.
Focus marketing and distribution efforts on Port Harcourt to improve revenue generation.
Increase promotion of high-profit products such as Minute Maid, Eva Water, and Diet Coke.
Continue optimizing Retail and Online channels, as they contribute significantly to total sales volume.
